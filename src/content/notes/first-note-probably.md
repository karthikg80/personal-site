I have built plenty of websites. Writing something personal on one feels different.

This is my first attempt. I do not have a voice for it yet—or maybe I do, and I have not heard it often enough to recognize it.

## what happened

- I redrew this site.
- The old version was tidy and professional.
- It also felt a little too much like a résumé.
- The new one has paper colours, red links, and a crooked drawing of a house.

The projects are still here. So are the identity pieces: my domain, RSS, and links to other places I appear online. The change is mostly that the site now has room for things that are not finished.

## an open question

I am not sure what belongs here yet.

Maybe notes about making small software. Things I noticed. Links worth keeping. A sentence that did not need to become an article.

I do not want a publishing schedule before I have a reason to publish.

## for now

One rough note.

Then I can see what the next one wants to be.
